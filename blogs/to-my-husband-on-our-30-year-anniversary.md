---
title: 'To my husband on our 30-year anniversary: thank you for not listening.'
date: 2026-07-04
thumbnail: husband-30-year-anniversary.jpeg
---

Thank you for not seeing.

Thank you for not caring.

Thank you for ignoring me.

Thank you for not listening when I asked you to stop taking so many photographs on our honeymoon. Thank you for not listening to my veiled admonitions, and sometimes my screaming. Thank you for turning a blind ear to my little girl whims. Thank you for not listening when I was being an entitled b@&$h. Thank you for not listening to me complain about my body and my food and all else that really did not matter. Thank you for always showing me another perspective and for being there every time I asked for help. And thank you for always putting music in the house even if I don’t always like it.

Thank you for not seeing how my body has changed and seeing only my soul. Thank you for not seeing the natural childbirths, my head in a showering cap and my shaving my legs. Thank you for showing me instead the beauty in a photograph or a piece of art. For showing me that not every minute of the next 10 years has to be pre-planned and controlled. Thank you for showing me how to just take it easy.

Thank you for not caring if I wanted to go out to dinner or on holiday without you. For not caring if I wanted to go to the gym at lunchtime. For not caring that I didn’t want to pursue a regular job and that I wanted to follow my dreams instead. Thank you for not caring if I had everything held together or not. Thank you for not caring if I made a wrong decision and got myself lost for a while. Thank you for always keeping the door back home open.

Thank you for ignoring me when I had doubts. For ignoring me when I hesitated. For ignoring me when I acted with fear and trepidation. Thank you for ignoring my failures and celebrating my successes as if they were yours. For shouting my name the loudest, and for always bringing me flowers.

Thank you for not listening. For not caring. For not seeing. For ignoring me. Thank you for giving me the room and the space to grow. In my own time. Thank you for your patience.

They say the most important decision we make is choosing the person we will marry. And here’s where I want to toot my own horn. Because I was young. But I was not stupid. I was inexperienced. But I was not unwise. And I could see, despite us coming from different countries and different upbringings, that we had a lot in common and that we would make an excellent team. I could understand, even then, that while all our circumstances could change, our characters won’t. Thank you for seeing the goodness in mine despite the hard shell.

I never thought we’d last this long but I’m glad we did.

Love,

Rana
