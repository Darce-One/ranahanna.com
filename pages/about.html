About Rana

Between places, always listening.

Are we born creative or made creative? Rana has spent her life asking questions like this one.

Born in Beirut, Lebanon, Rana lived there until the age of twelve. Growing up surrounded by books, travel, and a deep interest in acting, she became drawn to the freedom of inhabiting other lives—and eventually, to writing them.

Her work explores creativity, potential, and the liminal space between two points: between places, between versions of ourselves, and between loss and what can still be found.

Alongside writing, Rana works with students, helping them find their own direction and creative voice. She believes that resistance can point us toward what matters.
